Miqaat — Privacy Policy

Effective 29 September 2026 · Applies to the Miqaat Android app (com.usman.miqaat), Google Play edition

In short: Miqaat calculates prayer times on your device. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no Miqaat server. Nothing you enter is sent to the developer.

What the app uses, and why

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, analytics, tracking, profiling or crash-reporting services, and no advertising ID.
  • No accounts or sign-in, and no Miqaat server that could receive your data.
  • No access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or general storage. The only files it touches are ones you pick when you import a masjid timetable or restore a settings file.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data.
  • Location is never accessed in the background.

Backups and your control

  • Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for this app, so your location and settings are not copied by Google's backup.
  • Settings › Reliability & backup can write a settings file that you keep yourself, and restore it later. It is created only when you ask.
  • You can deny or revoke location, notification and alarm permissions at any time in Android settings. Times can then be set by choosing a place manually.
  • To delete everything Miqaat holds, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings. There is nothing on a server to delete.

Updates

The Google Play edition is updated by Google Play; the app itself makes no update requests. (A separate direct-download edition published on GitHub checks GitHub for new versions; that edition is not distributed through Google Play.)

Children

Miqaat is intended for people aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information off the device, there is nothing to collect from anyone.

Religious content

Miqaat shows hadith, adhkār and prayer instructions with their sources. Review status is stated in Settings › About › Content sources. This policy is about data only; it makes no religious ruling.

Open source and changes

The source code is public at github.com/haawas-alt/miqaat, so these statements can be checked. If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions, corrections or privacy requests: open an issue at github.com/haawas-alt/miqaat/issues. The developer contact email shown on the Google Play listing also reaches the developer.
