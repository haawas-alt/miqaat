## Corrections implemented

- Removed decorative Schedule icons from prayer cards. The single hero countdown icon remains.
- Shared measured name columns align all three prayer clocks in each grid column.
- Uniform worst-case clock fitting reserves a consistent NOW/NEXT lane; a status row cannot silently shrink only its own clock.
- Status badges reserve measured full-label width at supported font sizes.
- Full Hijri date, including AH, fits on one line without abbreviating the month.
- Kiswah band is width- and glyph-height-fitted, single-line, with diacritic/frame clearance.
- The reported 891×411 phone retains its stacked Kiswah hero; the hero is inset from the embroidered edge.
- Dark Miqaat/Celestial panels have stronger, quieter contrast.
- Gallery artwork/label lanes are compact enough to preserve clock hierarchy and measured name-to-clock clearance.
- Above 115% text size, production routing preserves the existing accessible, scrolling Home rather than forcing the dense board.

## Travel/timezone correction

- A saved automatic location near a known city no longer silently acquires a new device timezone when the device changes zones in flight. Explicit manual timezone choices remain authoritative.
- Beijing is included in the offline known-place set.
- Immediate cached-location reuse is limited to two minutes; fallback reuse is limited to fifteen minutes. Six-hour airport fixes and future-dated fixes are rejected.
- Regression tests reproduce saved Beijing coordinates on a Sydney-time device, assert the resolved Asia/Shanghai zone and broad sunrise/sunset windows, preserve manual overrides, and reject stale airport caches.
- These tests do not establish the cause of the user's device state or certify a fresh physical GPS acquisition.
