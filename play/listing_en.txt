TITLE: Miqaat: Prayer Times & Azaan
SHORT: Prayer times, azaan and Qibla calculated on your device. No ads, no tracking.
FULL:
Miqaat is a calm, accurate prayer-time and azaan app for your home tablet and your phone. Everything is calculated on your device: no account, no ads, no analytics, no server.

PRAYER TIMES YOU CAN CHECK
• Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, ʿAsr, Maghrib and ʿIshāʾ for anywhere in the world
• Eleven calculation methods, Shāfiʿī/Mālikī/Ḥanbalī or Ḥanafī ʿAsr, and high-latitude rules
• Tap the ⓘ on any prayer to see exactly how its time was worked out
• Monthly timetable, or import your own masjid's timetable with a row-by-row review before anything changes
• Disliked (makrūh) times for voluntary prayer, shown as an approximate guide

AZAAN THAT ARRIVES ON TIME
• Exact alarms, full-screen azaan with a clear Stop button, even with the screen off
• Optional iqamah countdown, reminders, and a dua after the azaan
• One hadith after each azaan, recited in Arabic and read in English
• Reliability screen that tells you if an azaan was ever late, and why

BUILT FOR THE WALL AND THE POCKET
• Two designs for a wall-mounted tablet: Miqaat (night sky and mihrab) and Kiswah (black and gold)
• Phone layouts for portrait and landscape, and a home-screen widget
• Large-type mode readable across a room
• Night dimming and keep-screen-on for a bedside or wall display

MORE
• Qibla compass with magnetic-declination correction and an accuracy indicator
• Hijri calendar (Umm al-Qura) with a moon-sighting adjustment
• Ramaḍān mode: suhoor, iftār, tarāwīḥ and the last third of the night
• Jumuʿah reminders and Friday sunnah
• Morning, evening and after-prayer adhkār
• Learn Ṣalāh: a step-by-step lesson with Arabic, transliteration, meaning and recitation, for beginners of any age
• English and Urdu

PRIVACY
Your location is used only to work out prayer times, and stays on your device. The only time anything leaves it is when you ask for a place name: Android's built-in geocoder may send the coordinates or your search text to Google. The source code is public at github.com/haawas-alt/miqaat.

ABOUT THE CONTENT
Every hadith, dhikr and ruling shows its source, and where scholars differ the app says so. The content has not yet been fully reviewed by a scholar; Settings › About › Content sources always shows exactly what has and has not been reviewed. Please confirm your local masjid's times, and use the correction link in About if you find a mistake.
